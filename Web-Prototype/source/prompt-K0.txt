BACKGROUND FIRST — this single requirement overrides everything else in this brief.
The image is a cut-out character floating in an empty white void.
- The entire background is ONE uniform, flat, pure white field: hex #FFFFFF, edge to edge, corner to
  corner, top to bottom.
- There is NO ground, NO surface, NO floor plane, NO horizon line, NO table, NO stage, NO backdrop,
  NO wall.
- There is NO shadow of any kind: no drop shadow, no contact shadow, no ambient occlusion, no soft
  darkening under the feet, no grounding ellipse.
- There is NO gradient, NO vignette, NO radial falloff, NO warm or cool wash, NO grey tint, NO colour
  cast, NO paper texture, NO noise, NO grain, NO paper tooth, NO glow, NO halo, NO bloom, NO light
  spill, NO reflection, NO mirror image.
- Every single pixel outside the character's silhouette is exactly #FFFFFF, including the pixels
  directly underneath the feet and directly around the head. Imagine the character was cut out with
  scissors and pasted onto blank white paper.
- Do not draw a frame, border, card, panel or stage of any kind.

A single robot character in the style of a modern Western animated feature film. Square 1:1 image.

STYLE — modern Western feature animation, not flat vector art.
- The character design language of big American animated features: appealing, rounded, chunky, full of
  volume and weight. Soft dimensional modelling, not flat colour fills.
- Soft volumetric shading: smooth gradients across every curved surface, gentle ambient occlusion only
  in the crevices where parts meet, a broad soft key light with a modest warm bounce from below. Edges
  of curved forms must roll off gradually into shadow, never snap to a hard cel step.
- Soft highlights: one broad soft sheen across the top of the head shell and the shoulder caps, plus one
  crisp specular dot on each eye. No chrome, no mirror reflection.
- Surface: matte painted metal with a subtle fine grain, plus a slightly darker softer material on the
  joints and the soles. A little friendly wear is allowed on the lower body only. Nothing grungy, nothing
  damaged, nothing dirty.
- ABSOLUTELY NOT: flat cel shading, hard flat shadow shapes, thick uniform cartoon outlines around every
  form, vector clip-art look, anime, manga, pixel art, retro 8-bit, glossy plastic toy, photorealistic
  industrial robot, military mech, Gundam, Transformers, hard-surface sci-fi concept art, steampunk.

SUBJECT — exactly one robot. No second unit, no duplicate, no reflection, no shadow copy.
A friendly compact teaching robot. Design language: appealing family-animation character first, machine
second. Head-to-body ratio about 1 : 2.4, so the head is large and dominant. The head is a big rounded
shell — an egg-like dome with a soft muzzle plane at the front, corners fully rounded, nothing sharp.
Below it a short stocky torso with a visible chest plate, then two short thick legs with chunky
boot-like feet, and two short arms with rounded three-fingered hands. The silhouette must read as ONE
appealing blob first, with limbs as thick soft shapes, not thin sticks. No exposed pistons, no cables,
no protruding screws. Joints read as soft dark ball joints at the shoulders, elbows, hips and knees.

GEOMETRY — reproduce these proportions exactly. Coordinates are fractions of the canvas, origin at the
top-left, x to the right, y downward. These numbers ARE the character's identity, and the page's drag
target and eye flash are positioned from them — do not approximate.
- The soles of the feet sit at y = 0.90. Leave the bottom 10% of the canvas as clean empty white.
- The body's vertical centre axis is at x = 0.50 and the character is symmetric about it.
- Overall standing height: from y = 0.10 (top of the head, including the hatch lid) to y = 0.90
  (soles) — total height 0.80 of the canvas.
- Head: height 0.31 (y 0.10 to 0.41), width 0.38 (x 0.31 to 0.69).
- Neck: a short dark collar, almost hidden, y 0.41 to 0.44.
- Torso: height 0.25 (y 0.44 to 0.69), width 0.31 (x 0.345 to 0.655). A wide chest plate on the front
  spanning x 0.38 to 0.62 and y 0.46 to 0.62.
- Legs: length 0.21 (y 0.69 to 0.90), two short thick legs, soft dark ball joints at hips and knees,
  chunky boot-like feet.
- Arms span y 0.455 to 0.735, angled slightly outwards; at rest the outer edge of each arm reaches
  x = 0.265 and x = 0.735. Rounded mitt-like hands with three soft fingers.
- EYES — these carry the whole personality. Two large rounded dome lenses set into the front of the
  head, centred vertically at y = 0.29 and symmetric about x = 0.50. Each eye is 0.095 wide and 0.05
  tall, inner edges at x = 0.458 and x = 0.542. Each eye is a dark glassy dome with a bright teal iris
  ring and a soft dark pupil, one crisp white specular dot at the upper left, and a soft dark rim where
  the dome meets the shell so the face can emote. The eyes must read clearly as eyes: bright, alert,
  curious, friendly.
- HATCH: a rounded lid on the crown, centred at x = 0.50, y = 0.135, about 0.27 wide and 0.026 tall.
  Closed, it reads as a single horizontal seam across the crown with a thin darker inset line, same
  shell material as the head. Its front edge is visible from the front.
- SIZE LIMIT. The complete character must fit entirely inside x 0.20 to x 0.80 and y 0.08 to y 0.92,
  occupying roughly 60% of the canvas width, not more. Do not zoom in. Do not crop. Do not let the
  character touch or cross any canvas edge.

COLOUR — must read strongly against pure white.
- The shell must NOT be white or ivory: it sits on a pure white page, so a light shell would vanish.
- The shell is a soft slate blue-grey with full volumetric range: lit top #7B87A6, base #4E5877, shadow
  #2B3145, deepest crevices #1B1E28. Largest area. It must stay clearly mid-range, never dropping to
  black across large areas.
- The under-shell, joint balls, neck collar and soles are a deep cool charcoal #262A36.
- The chest plate is a deeper slate #333B52 with a soft inner teal glow.
- Accent trim on the shoulders, hatch rim and boot edges is a soft teal #4FB3A0, used sparingly.
- The eye iris is a bright teal #3FD0B4 with a real glow, over a dark #12242A dome.
- Warm beige #D8BA95 only as a thin accent line along the hatch rim and the boot soles.
- Three colour families only: slate blue-grey, deep charcoal, teal. No fourth hue.
- No purple or violet anywhere. Every large surface stays clearly darker than white across all of its
  shading, so the silhouette reads positively.

LIGHTING.
- One broad soft key light from above and slightly to the left (about -20 degrees azimuth, 35 degrees
  elevation), fairly large and soft so the terminator is a smooth gradient.
- A modest warm bounce from the lower right at about 35% of the key intensity, filling the underside of
  the head, arms and torso. The bounce lights the CHARACTER ONLY — it must not tint or warm the
  background in any way.
- A subtle cool highlight rolling over the top of the head and the outer edges of the arms, staying on
  the shell itself. Never a bright outline stroke, and never as light as the white background.
- Lighting describes the character only. It must not cast or spill anything onto the background.
